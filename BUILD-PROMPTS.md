## Phase 1 — Research & positioning
```

Report: (1) any existing uses or trademarks of 222248; (2) the search demand behind "2222" and "48 hours"
(48-hour rule, 48-hour work week, 48-hour launch/hackathon); (3) three monetizable site concepts with
estimated RPM tier, lead value and competition. Pick ONE and justify with numbers.
```
