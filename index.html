You can launch a first version and get real market feedback in 48 hours. That means a clear offer, a landing page, a way to pay and a first wave of visitors — not a finished company.

Yes. The planner, calculators and guides are free. The site is supported by advertising, sponsors and voluntary donations.

What does the done-for-you launch include?

We scope your idea, build the landing page and core flow, wire forms, payments and analytics, and prepare a launch plan. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

How does the monthly challenge work?

Each month a theme is announced on a Friday. You have 48 hours to build and submit. Entries are judged on problem clarity, execution and traction.
