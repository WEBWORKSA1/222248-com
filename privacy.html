Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

This policy explains what information 222248.com (“we”, “us”) collects, how we use it and your choices.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (contact, newsletter, launch brief, challenge registration, talent application, donation pledge or advertising inquiry) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service. We use this information only to respond to you and provide what you requested.

Information stored in your browser

The planner, theme preference and cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser only.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalised ads are used, as required by law.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. YouTube may then set cookies under its own policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (hosting, form delivery, payment providers for donations) or when required by law.

Retention

We keep inquiries and applications as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, then delete them. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your information, and to withdraw consent. Make a request through our contact form (choose “Privacy request”).

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
