About

Why 2222 · 48?

Because the best way to learn whether a business works is to launch it — and 48 hours is enough to find out.

What we do

222248.com is an independent resource for people who build online businesses with modern AI tools. We publish free planners, calculators and practical guides, run a monthly 48-hour build challenge, and offer a done-for-you launch service for people who want the result without the weekend.

The name

Read the domain as “2222 · 48”: a repeating, memorable number followed by the 48 hours at the heart of our method. The number is used purely as a web address and brand identifier; we make no claim of exclusive rights to it. See our trademark & copyright disclosure.

Editorial policy

  • Guides are written to be original and practical, and are reviewed for accuracy before publishing and when updated.
  • We cite official sources for legal and regulatory topics and label general information clearly.
  • Advertising and sponsorships never decide what we write. Sponsored placements are labeled.
  • Corrections: if you spot an error, tell us and we will fix it.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, the done-for-you launch service, and voluntary support from readers. The tools stay free.
