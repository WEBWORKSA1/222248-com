How to Monetize a Content Site: Ads, Affiliates, Lead Generation and Sponsors

By the 222248 editorial team · Updated 2026-10-26 · 10 min read

A content site can earn money in four main ways. Each rewards a different kind of traffic, and the strongest sites combine them so that every visitor has at least one path to value.

1. Display advertising

Ad networks such as Google AdSense pay you when ads on your pages are viewed or clicked. Income is usually discussed as RPM — revenue per thousand page views. RPM varies widely by topic, country and season: commercial topics such as software, finance and business generally earn more than entertainment. Ads are the easiest model to start and scale with traffic, but they need volume.

To get approved and stay approved, a site needs original, useful content, clear navigation, an about page, a contact route, a privacy policy and compliance with the network's program policies. Never click your own ads or ask others to.

2. Affiliate marketing

You recommend products and earn a commission on sales through your links. It works best on pages where readers are deciding what to buy: comparisons, reviews, “best tools for X.” Disclose affiliate relationships clearly and only recommend what you would stand behind.

3. Lead generation

You collect enquiries from people who need a service and either deliver it yourself or pass qualified leads to a partner for a fee. One qualified lead can be worth more than thousands of ad views, which makes lead generation the highest-leverage model for business topics. The keys are a specific offer, a short form and fast follow-up.

4. Sponsorships and memberships

Once you have an engaged audience, companies pay for placements in your newsletter, events or challenges, and readers may support you directly through donations or memberships. These grow with trust rather than raw traffic.

Estimate your income

The website revenue estimator combines all four streams. Enter monthly page views, an assumed RPM, your affiliate click rate and earnings per click, and expected leads with their value. Use conservative inputs and treat the result as a planning range, not a forecast.

A combined model

  • Guides and tools attract search traffic and earn ad revenue.
  • Comparison sections earn affiliate commissions.
  • A clear “get it done for you” form turns the most motivated readers into leads.
  • A newsletter and community create sponsorship inventory.

Ad revenue rewards pages people find useful enough to stay on. Write for the reader first; the monetization follows.
