Your First 100 Users: Launch Channels That Work Without an Ad Budget

By the 222248 editorial team · Updated 2026-10-26 · 8 min read

Nobody finds a new product by accident. Your first users come from places you deliberately show up, with something useful to say. Here is how to do that without a budget.

Start with warm contacts

The people you interviewed during validation, your existing network and anyone who signed up to your smoke-test page are the warmest audience you have. Send each a personal message, not a broadcast. Ask for honest feedback, not favours.

Communities where buyers gather

Every niche has forums, groups and chat communities. Read their rules first. Most welcome a genuine story — what problem you saw, what you built, what you learned — and reject drive-by promotion. Lead with insight; mention the product once.

Launch directories

Product launch sites and niche directories can bring a burst of early adopters and backlinks. Prepare a clear tagline, a short demo or screenshots, and be present on launch day to answer every comment.

Content that keeps working

Write one genuinely helpful article that answers a question your buyers search for. Unlike social posts, search content compounds. Link it to your product naturally.

Direct outreach

For business products, a short, specific message to the right person often beats everything else. Mention something real about them, state the problem you solve in one sentence, and ask a simple question.
