Official rules (summary)

  1. Eligibility: open to individuals aged 18 or older where such contests are lawful. Void where prohibited. Organisers and their families may not win prizes.
  2. Skill-based: winners are selected by judges using the published criteria. No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win.
  3. Original work: entries must be built during the 48-hour window. Open-source libraries and AI tools are allowed; disclose major pre-existing components.
  4. Ownership: you keep all rights to your project. You grant 222248.com permission to feature your entry's name, screenshots and description.
  5. Prizes: announced with each theme. Prizes are non-transferable; winners are responsible for any taxes. Prizes may require identity and eligibility verification.
  6. Conduct: no illegal, harmful, hateful or infringing projects. We may disqualify entries that break these rules.
  7. Changes: we may modify or cancel a challenge if circumstances require; registered participants will be notified.
