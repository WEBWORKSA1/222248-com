# 222248 — Research Summary

## The number
- **222248 has no notable standalone meaning** in commerce, AI, economics or culture. Web searches return only incidental matches (star-catalogue coordinates, unrelated codes). Search demand for "222248" itself is effectively zero — so the site must not depend on the number.
- **2222** carries heavy consumer search interest as an "angel number" (balance, partnership) and as the year/time 22:22 — high volume, low commercial value, and AdSense-sensitive (spiritual/occult content tends to earn low RPM).
- **48** carries strong *business* intent: the 48-hour decision rule, the EU Working Time Directive's 48-hour average week (and similar limits in Ontario and elsewhere), 48-hour sprints/hackathons, 48-hour launches.

## Decision
Read the domain as **"2222 · 48"** and own the "48 hours" concept: **Launch in 48 — launch an AI-powered online business in 48 hours.**

Why this beats the alternatives (angel-number site, number-trivia site, generic tools site):
| Factor | Launch in 48 | Angel-number content |
|---|---|---|
| Ad category | Business / software / AI / finance (high RPM tier) | Spirituality (low RPM tier) |
| Lead value | Done-for-you launch $300–$5,000 per client | None |
| Sponsor inventory | Monthly challenge, newsletter, featured tools | Weak |
| AdSense approval risk | Low (original how-to + tools) | Higher (thin, repetitive content is common) |
| Brand fit with number | "48" hook is literal and memorable | Only "2222" fits |

Revenue engine: guides + calculators earn search traffic and AdSense; the multi-step "Get Launched" form converts motivated readers into service leads; the monthly challenge creates sponsorship and newsletter growth; donations fund prizes and operations.

## Benchmark — 30 sites reviewed (Oct 2026)
Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, Starter Story, YC Apply, Startup School, Failory, MicroConf, Devpost, MLH, Startup Weekend, There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Toolify, Omni Calculator, Acquire.com, Flippa, Gumroad, Shopify Blog, Stripe Atlas, HubSpot for Startups, NerdWallet Business, Entrepreneur, SBA.gov, RemoteOK, Nomads.com, Exploding Topics, Kickstarter, Buy Me a Coffee, Ideabrowser.

Patterns adopted on 222248.com:
- Founder-stage organisation (validate → build → launch → monetize), as Shopify, SBA and MicroConf do.
- Calculators with live results, the formula shown and cross-links, as Omni Calculator does.
- A quiz-style multi-step lead form, as Futurepedia and Stripe Atlas do.
- Deadline countdown, rules, judging weights and separate participant/sponsor/judge paths, as Devpost, MLH and Startup Weekend do.
- Preset donation amounts, monthly tiers and a public supporter opt-in, as Buy Me a Coffee does.
- Clearly labelled sponsored and featured placements, as Product Hunt and There's An AI For That do.
- Newsletter capture in several places, as Failory and Starter Story do.
- A talent pool with an employer path, as RemoteOK does.

Backlog (Phase 9): searchable case-study database, challenge leaderboard and gallery, "was this helpful" votes, programmatic calculator pages.
